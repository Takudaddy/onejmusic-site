Nice Picture

Simone D.B · Debut Single · ONE-J Music

A cynical hip-hop track about a society obsessed with appearances, where reality is constantly edited to look good and people eventually become trapped in the images they've created. Driven by raw beats and dry, understated rap, Nice Picture questions the line between who we are and who we pretend to be.
